AgriTrace is a low-cost IoT traceability platform that follows produce from the farm all the way to the consumer. As a batch moves through harvesting, collection, transport, storage and retail, each stage adds a verified record to its journey — so anyone, at any point in the chain, can see exactly where a batch has been, how it was handled, and whether it stayed within safe conditions along the way.

Full journey visibility
Farm, collection, transport, storage, distribution and retail — one continuous, shared record.
Tamper-evident records
Every record is linked to the one before it, so any change to the history is immediately detectable.
Real-time alerts
Cold-chain breaks and handling issues are flagged the moment a sensor reading crosses a safe limit.
